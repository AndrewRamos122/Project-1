Dueling Strategies

How I prepare a deck

  1. Choosing a theme or archetype that I enjoy.
  2. Decide on a clear win condition.
  3. Test the deck and remove cards that do not support the deck.
  4. Practice going first and going second.
  5. Build a 40 card deck to have consistency.

Current confidence:

65 out of 100

The most important part is to alway have fun.

Beginner vocabulary

ED: cards that support a deck from outside the Main Deck called Extra Deck.

SS: a summon performed by a card effect or game mechanic, known as Special Summon.
